ModernSecure is the client and staff workspace of TOO "Sovremennaya Zashchita", a legal services company in Kazakhstan (BIN 220140025162). Version 1.0.1 (4) updates the company icon and interface while retaining the existing app identifier.

The app provides assigned cases, stages, tasks, document requests, uploads/downloads and case conversations. Access depends on account activation and role. The iOS container provides back gestures, system file/camera selection and native Quick Look document previews. An internet connection is required. The hosted workspace is https://modern-secure.vercel.app. Push delivery is not configured or advertised for this release.

Use the activated client credentials in the private review fields. This account is isolated to the synthetic case "ДЕМО APPLE — проверка приложения, не юридическая услуга" (MS-2026-000007). Required terms and privacy consent have been accepted for this demo account only. Use non-sensitive test attachments and messages. New registrations require email confirmation and company activation.

Account deletion can be requested inside the app through Profile → Delete account or the Delete account link below the login form, including before company activation. No phone call or separate email is required to initiate the request. The company processes requests within the published maximum of 30 calendar days, subject to legal-retention exceptions, and sends confirmation. Request submission does not immediately erase case records.
